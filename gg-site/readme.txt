=== GG Site ===
Companion plugin for the GG Theme (Garg Garg & Associates website).

Adds: Services, Team, Due dates and Enquiries in the dashboard; calculators;
the Acts & Rules pages; the appointment form; Settings → GG Site (phone, email,
address, Office Staff Login link); Tools → GG Site Setup (one-click setup).

Shortcodes: [gg_calculators] [gg_calculator type="gst"] [gg_due_dates]
[gg_hub head="gst"] [gg_hub_index] [gg_services] [gg_team] [gg_portal_links]
[gg_enquiry]
